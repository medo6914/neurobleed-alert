# Go-to-Market Strategy — NeuroBleed Alert v1.0

---

## Phase 1: Clinical Proof (Months 1–8)

**Objective**: Build software platform and validate clinically.

| Activity | Timeline | Owner |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Complete software platform (Flutter + Backend + AI) | Months 1–6 | Engineering |
| Identify 3 pilot hospital partners | Month 3 | CEO/Founder |
| Establish Medical Advisory Board (3 neurosurgeons) | Month 3 | CEO/Founder |
| Submit IRB protocol for clinical validation study | Month 4 | Clinical |
| Begin data collection for AI model training | Month 5 | AI Team |
| Publish pre-print on AI model performance | Month 7 | AI Team |

**First Customers**: Approach 3 government tertiary hospitals in KSA with existing neurosurgery departments.
- **KFSH (King Faisal Specialist Hospital)** — Riyadh
- **KAMC (King Abdulaziz Medical City)** — Jeddah
- **KFSH (King Fahad Specialist Hospital)** — Dammam

**Value Prop for Pilots**: Free 6-month pilot. No cost. We handle all setup. You get:
- Continuous ICH monitoring for your highest-risk patients
- Real-time mobile alerts for your neurosurgery team
- No additional nursing burden
- Full Arabic interface

## Go-to-Market Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Hospital sales cycles too long | Start with free pilots — zero procurement friction |
| Regulatory roadblocks | Begin SFDA process early (Month 12); engage regulatory consultant |
| Competitor responses | ICH-specific focus creates defensible niche |
| Clinical adoption resistance | Medical Advisory Board champions at target hospitals |
| Pricing pressure | Tiered pricing by hospital size; value-based pricing tied to outcomes |
