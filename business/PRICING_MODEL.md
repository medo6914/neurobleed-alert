# Pricing Model — NeuroBleed Alert

---

## Pricing Philosophy

Value-based pricing tied to clinical outcomes (reduced CT scans, reduced ICU days, reduced mortality). Target: 10× cheaper than incumbent ICU monitoring systems while delivering superior ICH-specific value.

---

## Model 1: Hospital Subscription (Per Bed)

**Best for**: Government hospitals, large ICUs (20+ beds)

| Tier | Monthly/Bed | Annual Commitment | Features |
|------|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Basic** | $200/bed/month | $2,400/bed/year | 1 device included, cloud monitoring, mobile alerts, email support |
| **Professional** | $350/bed/month | $4,200/bed/year | 2 devices included, AI risk scores, SHAP explanations, reports, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | $500/bed/month | $6,000/bed/year | Unlimited devices, FHIR/HL7 integration, API access, dedicated support, SLA |

**Example**: 20-bed ICU at Professional tier = $7,000/month = $84,000/year.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Features |
|------|---------|--------|----------|

**Example**: 10 devices + hospital subscription = $25,000 + $24,000/year = **$49,000 first year**, $24,000/year renewal.
